FAQ

What does 520 mean in Chinese?

520 (wǔ èr líng) sounds like 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ), “I love you.” 20 May (5/20) is celebrated as an online Valentine's Day in China.

What does 1314 mean?

1314 (yī sān yī sì) sounds like 一生一世, “one life, one world,” i.e. forever. It is common in wedding dates and gift amounts, and the 4 reads positively here.

Is 7 lucky or unlucky in Chinese?

Mixed. 七 sounds like 起 (rise) and 齐 (together), which are positive, but the 7th lunar month is the Ghost Month, so some people avoid 7 for weddings and moving.

What does 88 mean in texting?

88 (bā bā) sounds like “bye-bye.” Outside chat it means double prosperity, 发发.

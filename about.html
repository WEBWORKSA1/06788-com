About 06788

A modern, practical home for one of the world's most widely shared traditions: numbers that bring luck.

Why we built it

For more than a billion people, a number is more than a quantity. It's a phone line that sounds prosperous, a plate worth millions, a wedding date that gets everyone off to a good start. Yet most information online is scattered, dated or vague. 06788 turns that tradition into precise, free tools with clear explanations.

How the tools work

  • Analyzer: transparent digit weights, 40+ documented combinations and pattern detection.
  • Lucky dates: the standard almanac Twelve Day Officers, with day pillars calculated from the Julian Day Number and solar-term tables for 2024–2032.
  • Zodiac: exact Chinese New Year dates, 1930–2031.

How we're funded

Ads (clearly labelled), sponsorships, Prosperity Desk referrals and reader support. Editorial content isn't for sale.

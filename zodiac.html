FAQ

What is the Chinese zodiac animal for 2026?

2026 is the Year of the Fire Horse (丙午), running from 17 February 2026 to 5 February 2027.

Which signs clash with the Horse in 2026?

The Rat is the Horse's opposite sign and is said to “clash with Tai Sui.” Horse-born people are in their own year (本命年) and traditionally wear red for protection.

What is 2027's zodiac?

2027 is the Year of the Fire Goat (丁未), starting 6 February 2027. The Horse and Goat are a Six Harmonies pair, so the handover is considered smooth.

I was born in January. Which animal am I?

If you were born before that year's Chinese New Year, you belong to the previous animal. The calculator above handles this automatically.

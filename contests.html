Official rules (summary)

  1. Free to enter. No purchase necessary. One entry per person per month.
  2. Open worldwide where not prohibited by law. Void where prohibited.
  3. Entries must be your own photos. Don't include faces or private data without consent, and blur full licence plates of other people's vehicles if local law requires it.
  4. Judging: 50% Analyzer score, 50% creativity and story, by the 06788 team. Decisions are final.
  5. Winners are notified by email and must reply within 14 days. Prizes are paid by PayPal or gift card. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  6. By entering you grant 06788.com a non-exclusive licence to show your entry on the site and social channels with credit.
  7. The organiser may change or cancel a round if the contest is compromised. Personal data is handled under our Privacy Policy.

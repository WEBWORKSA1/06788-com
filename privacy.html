Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What we collect

  • Tool inputs (numbers, dates, birth years) are processed in your browser and are not sent to us, unless you submit them in a form.
  • Form submissions (name, email, message and details you choose to give) are sent to our team inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-relay service, along with the page URL and campaign parameters (UTM) where present.
  • Local preferences (theme, consent choice, last birth year) are stored in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

Cookies & advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. If you decline in our consent banner, we request non-personalised ads only. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or at aboutads.info. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits.

How we use data

We use it to reply to requests, deliver what you asked for (reports, calendars, newsletters), run contests, connect Prosperity Desk requests with a relevant specialist only with your consent, and improve the site. We never sell personal data.

Retention & security

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to handle them and for our legitimate records (usually up to 24 months). Data travels over HTTPS.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, PDPA and others), you may access, correct, delete or export your data and object to processing. Use the contact form to make a request.

Children

The site is general-audience and not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new date.

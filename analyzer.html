How the Prosperity Score works

Every digit has a traditional weight based on what it sounds like in Mandarin and Cantonese. 8 (发, prosper) scores highest, then 6 (顺, smooth) and 9 (久, lasting). 4 (死, death) scores lowest. The last digit counts extra, because it's the one people remember.

  • Combinations such as 168 (一路发, “prosperity all the way”), 518 (我要发, “I will prosper”) and 1314 (一生一世, “forever”) add large bonuses. Negative ones such as 14 (要死), 74 (气死) and 250 (a fool) subtract.
  • Collector patterns (AAAA runs, ABAB repeats, ABBA mirrors, AABB pairs, rising sequences and palindromes) raise the market tier.
  • Zodiac match: add a birth year and we check how many digits are traditional lucky numbers for your animal.

FAQ

Does the analyzer work for non-Chinese phone numbers?

Yes. The meanings come from sound, so any digit string works: US, UK, Canadian, Indian, Singaporean, Hong Kong or mainland numbers, plates and prices.

Should I include the country code?

Score the part people see and dial. Most people judge the last 8 digits, and the ending counts most.

My number has a 4. Is it bad?

It lowers the score, but context matters. In 1314 (一生一世, forever) and 3344 (生生世世, lifetime after lifetime) the 4 reads positively, and the analyzer takes that into account.

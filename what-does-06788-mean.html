What does 06788 mean?

A five-digit string that Chinese speakers read as a blessing: 你顺起发发, “You rise smoothly into double prosperity.”

Updated September 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: 0 = 你 (you), 6 = 顺/流 (smooth), 7 = 起 (rise), 88 = 发发 (double prosperity). 06788 has no 4 and ends on a doubled 8, so it scores 94/99 on our analyzer.

How Chinese number puns work

Mandarin and Cantonese have many homophones, so digits easily stand in for words. 520 means “I love you”, 88 means “bye-bye” in chat, and 8 means 发, “prosper”. Read a string as a phrase and you have a message, a blessing or occasionally a curse.

The tail 678 (顺起发, “smoothly rise to prosperity”) and 788 (起发发, “rise to double prosperity”) are auspicious on their own. Together they build a phrase that moves forward, from you, through smooth progress and rising, to wealth.

Why buyers value strings like this

  • No 4. 四 sounds like 死 (death), and buyers screen it out first.
  • Ends on 8. The last digit is the one people remember and repeat.
  • Doubled 8. 88 doubles the prosperity reading and is easy to say.
  • Short. Five digits fit a phone ending, a plate, a price or a domain.

The market takes this seriously. Hong Kong's plate “28” (易发, easy prosperity) sold for HK$18.1 million in 2016, and on Singapore marketplaces “golden” 8888 phone numbers are listed from S$88 up to six figures.

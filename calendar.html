The officer comes from the relationship between the day's Earthly Branch and the solar month (which begins at the solar terms, such as 立春 Start of Spring). Day pillars are calculated from the Julian Day Number. Solar-term dates for 2024–2032 are built in.

FAQ

What is the luckiest day to open a business?

Traditionally a 成 (Success), 开 (Open), 满 (Full) or 定 (Stable) day that doesn't clash with the owner's zodiac sign. Many owners also pick a date containing 8.

Why is a day marked red for me but green for others?

Every day clashes with one animal (the day's opposite branch). If it's yours, the day is marked red for you even when its officer is good.

How accurate is this calendar?

Day pillars and officers match the standard almanac for 2024–2032. It is a cultural guide, not a guarantee. For weddings and major launches many families also consult a master.

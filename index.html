Quick answers

What does 06788 mean in Chinese?

In number slang 0 reads as 你 (you), 6 as 顺/流 (smooth), 7 as 起 (rise) and 8 as 发 (prosper). Together: “You rise smoothly into double prosperity.” It contains no 4 and ends on a doubled 8, the two things buyers value most.

Why is 8 the luckiest number?

八 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā), “to prosper.” Prices, plates, phone numbers and even the Beijing 2008 Olympics start time (8:08:08 pm on 8/8/08) were chosen for it.

Why do Chinese speakers avoid 4?

四 (sì) sounds like 死 (sǐ), “death.” Many buildings skip 4th, 14th and 24th floors, and numbers such as 14 (要死) and 74 (气死) are avoided.

How is the Prosperity Score calculated?

Each digit carries a traditional weight (8 highest, 4 lowest). The final digit counts extra. Famous combinations (168, 518, 1314…) and collector patterns (AAAA, ABAB, mirrors, sequences) add bonuses, and an optional birth year adds a zodiac match.

No. 06788 is cultural entertainment and education. For buying or selling valuable numbers, use the Prosperity Desk to reach a specialist.

# 06788.com — Research Brief

## 1. What "06788" means in Chinese culture

| Digit | Mandarin | Sound-alike (谐音) | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | líng 零 | 你 nǐ ("you") in number slang; 零 = "from zero / start" | Neutral → "you" / a fresh start |
| 6 | liù 六 | 溜 / 流 liū/liú — "smooth, flowing"; 六六大顺 "everything goes smoothly" | Lucky (business, travel) |
| 7 | qī 七 | 起 qǐ — "rise, get up"; also 齐 "together" | Context-dependent; positive in "rise" readings, cautious in ghost-month contexts |
| 8 | bā 八 | 发 fā — "get rich, prosper" (发财) | Luckiest single digit |
| 88 | bā bā | 发发 "double prosperity"; 拜拜 "bye-bye" in chat; 双喜-style doubling | Very lucky |

**Popular reading of 06788:** 你·顺·起·发发 — *"You rise smoothly into double prosperity."*
The string reads as a blessing. It has no 4 (死, "death") and ends on a doubled 8, and Chinese-speaking buyers value those two features most.

### Economic importance of lucky numbers (the market the site sits in)
- Hong Kong plate auctions (Transport Dept.): "W" HK$26M (2021), "R" HK$25.5M (2023), "H" HK$20M (Mar 2026), "28" HK$18.1M (2016), "18" HK$16.5M (2008), "9" HK$13M (1994), "8" HK$5M (1988).
- The 2008 Beijing Olympics opened at 8:08:08 pm on 8/8/08.
- Retail prices in Chinese markets often end in 8, and premium "golden" phone numbers (8888, AAAA/ABAB patterns) sell from S$88 to S$588,888 on Singapore marketplaces.
- Carriers charge prepayments for "pretty numbers". Businesses choose opening dates, prices, floors, addresses and domains by number.

### Trademark and identity check
- The 5-digit leading-zero format looks like a HKEX stock code. A search found no active HKEX issuer on 06788. China Tower is 00788; LianLian DigiTech is 02598. Unrelated tickers "6788" exist on the Tokyo (Nihon Trim) and Taipei OTC (Brillian Network) exchanges.
- The site uses "06788" only as a generic numeric string and cultural expression. It never presents itself as a ticker, never uses a listed company's name or logo, and carries a full non-affiliation disclosure.

## 3. Positioning decision
**06788.com: the Prosperity Number Desk.** A lucky-number analysis and prosperity-planning hub for people and businesses. It lets visitors score, choose and value lucky numbers and dates for phones, plates, addresses, prices, launches and gifts.

Why this beats the alternatives:
1. **Commercial intent.** "Lucky number" searches lead to purchases (plates, phone numbers, domains, opening dates, pricing). That gives better lead value than a pure horoscope site.
2. **Evergreen traffic plus seasonal spikes.** Chinese New Year, weddings, the Mid-Autumn Festival and HK plate auctions bring recurring peaks.
3. **The domain name is its own story.** 06788 means "you rise smoothly into double prosperity", so the brand pitches itself.
4. **Several revenue streams:** AdSense, YouTube, lead brokerage (plate/number/domain valuation, date and feng shui consults, China-market pricing), sponsorships, affiliates (red envelopes, décor), donations and supporter tiers.

Differentiation from number-slang decoders: this site is about **money, luck and decisions** (score it, time it, price it, value it), not internet language.
